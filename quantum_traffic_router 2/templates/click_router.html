Click the map, or search a place above, to drop a Start point, then an End point, then any stops in between (up to {{ max_stops }} total). Drag a pin to move it, click a pin to remove it.     Per-stop weights

    Give each stop a delivery weight (or volume — any consistent unit works). "Max load/vehicle" above then caps the TOTAL weight a vehicle carries, not its stop count. Any stop left blank defaults to 1.

      Precedence rules

      Require one stop to be visited before another — e.g. a pickup before its matching drop-off. This is a real constraint baked directly into the solve, not a post-hoc filter (see the README). Single-vehicle mode only; rules automatically follow your stops as the route is (re-)solved, but are cleared if you add or remove a stop, since positions shift.
